Most of what a claim pays for is decided before you call them.

Once an adjuster has been out and written their number, changing it is hard. What gets found and photographed first is what the whole thing rests on.

Before you file

Get it inspected first

  • Full inspection, photographed, before an adjuster is involved
  • Hail bruising and wind lift documented — the damage that gets walked past
  • A written report you can hand to your insurer

Straight Answers

The questions people actually ask

“Do you work with my insurance?”

All insurance companies accepted. Every insurer has its own process — we know them all and handle the paperwork so you do not have to learn it.

“Are you licensed and insured?”

Yes. On a live site the licence number would sit here, with a link so you can check it yourself rather than take our word for it.

“How much does a new roof cost?”

It depends on size, pitch and material — which is why the inspection is free and the price comes in writing afterwards, not over the phone.

“How long does it take?”

Most residential replacements are a single day on site. Insurance work depends on the adjuster, and we will tell you what to expect.
